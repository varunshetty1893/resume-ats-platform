About Zentra

Built for the gap between a good resume and a good hire.

Zentra started with a simple frustration: qualified people getting filtered out before a human ever reads their resume. We built the tools to close that gap, for candidates and recruiters alike.

Our mission

Precision over guesswork, on both sides of the table.

Over 75% of resumes never reach a human reviewer — they're filtered out by applicant tracking systems on keyword and formatting mismatches alone. Meanwhile recruiters wade through hundreds of applications to find the handful that actually fit.

Zentra applies the same AI-driven matching to both sides: candidates get a clear, actionable read on how their resume will actually perform, and recruiters get a shortlist that's already been filtered for genuine fit.
