Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How does the ATS score calculation work?

Zentra's ATS engine compares your resume against the job requirements using skill keyword density, experience relevance, role alignment, and section completeness. A score of 70% or higher indicates strong alignment.

What happens when an application deadline passes?

Once a job deadline expires, the job automatically stops accepting new submissions. Existing candidates can still track their active application, and recruiters can review submissions or extend the deadline if needed.

How will I be notified when support responds to my ticket?

You will receive an in-app notification in your top navbar notification bell. Clicking the notification opens the ticket directly where you can read the response and reply.

Can I attach files or screenshots to my ticket?

Yes! You can attach screenshots or documents (PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX up to 5MB) when opening a ticket or replying to an existing one.
