What we collect

Account details (name, email, password hash), resume content you upload or paste, and job application activity (which jobs you applied to, and your match scores).

How it's used

Your resume and profile data are used to compute ATS scores and job matches, and are shared with a recruiter only when you apply to their listing.

Data you control

You can update your profile at any time from Settings. Contact support to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Third parties

We don't sell your data. Resume text is processed locally by Zentra's own matching engine — it isn't sent to third-party services.
